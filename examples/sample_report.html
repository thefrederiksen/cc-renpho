Findings

2 high, 2 medium, 3 low.

HIGH · composition-not-measured

Body-composition figures were not measured on most records

30 of 36 records (83%) carry resistance = 100, the placeholder the scale writes when it takes no impedance reading. Across them there are 28 distinct weights and 0 case(s) where the same weight yielded different composition.

Why it matters Body fat, muscle, water, protein and bone on those records are a formula over weight, height, age and sex - not a measurement. Two people of the same weight, height, age and sex would get identical figures. Nothing in the API response marks them as derived. Treat them as a restatement of weight.

HIGH · too-sparse-for-trend

The record is too sparse to support a trend

36 readings on 35 distinct days across 575 days - 6.09% coverage. The densest run holds 31 of them (86%).

Why it matters A trend line over data this sparse describes where the sampling happened to land, not what the body did. Densely sampled stretches can be read; isolated points cannot be joined to them.

MEDIUM · timestamp-field-offset

The timeStamp field is 8 hours off UTC

On 36 of 36 records, createdAt minus utcfromtimestamp(timeStamp) is 8 hours. Renpho's backend stamps the epoch in UTC+8, not UTC.

Why it matters Code that reads the raw epoch places every weigh-in 8 hours early, which moves some of them onto the previous day. Use localCreatedAt (local wall clock) or createdAt (true UTC) instead.

MEDIUM · backfilled-records

Some readings were backfilled, not recorded live

2025-04-09 (81.7 kg) arrived at 2025-09-27 12:44:03

Why it matters These reached the server long after the date they claim, so their timing rests entirely on the app rather than on when the reading arrived. Treat their dates as asserted, not corroborated.

LOW · repeat-weigh-ins

Some weigh-ins are repeats of the same session

1 pair(s) of readings less than 10 minutes apart - someone stepping on the scale twice, not two observations.

Why it matters They inflate the reading count without adding information. Count distinct days, not records, when reporting how much data there is.

LOW · inconsistent-timezone-label

The timezone label is inconsistent

Labels present: -5:00 (22), EST (13), -4:00 (1)

Why it matters The label is written by whichever app version uploaded the record, and mixes abbreviations with numeric offsets. It is not a reliable zone identifier - the local and UTC timestamps are.

LOW · empty-fields

A large part of the payload carries no data

18 of 48 fields are empty on every record. 0 field(s) are missing from some records entirely.

Why it matters These reflect hardware the scale does not have and health services that are not connected. They are schema, not data - do not model them as nullable measurements.
